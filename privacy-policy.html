Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you use our website.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to KUSHINARA CONNECT. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information may be handled when you visit or use our website.

This Privacy Policy explains the general practices of KUSHINARA CONNECT regarding information, cookies, advertising, communication and third-party services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide.

  • Name or other details submitted through an enquiry form.
  • Email address when you contact us by email.
  • Phone or WhatsApp number when you contact us directly.
  • Information submitted by businesses for listing or enquiries.

We only intend to use information for legitimate purposes connected with providing, improving or communicating about our website and services.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and messages.
  • Provide requested information or assistance.
  • Improve the website and user experience.
  • Manage business or service enquiries.
  • Maintain website security and functionality.

4. Cookies

KUSHINARA CONNECT may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage and support relevant services.

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, although some website features may not function as intended.

5. Google Advertising

We may use Google and other third-party advertising services on KUSHINARA CONNECT.

These services may use cookies or similar technologies to display advertisements based on a user's visit to this website or other websites.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on this website, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable settings, user choices and regional requirements.

Users may have options to manage advertising personalization through Google's available advertising settings and controls.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links, services or integrations provided by third parties, including hotels, restaurants, transport providers, businesses, maps, messaging services, analytics providers or advertising platforms.

Third-party websites and services have their own privacy policies and terms. KUSHINARA CONNECT is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites that you may access through links on our website.

If you choose to contact KUSHINARA CONNECT through WhatsApp, email or another communication method, the information you provide may be used to respond to your enquiry.

WhatsApp and email are third-party communication services and may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Businesses may provide information for listing, promotion or communication purposes.

Business owners are responsible for ensuring that information submitted by them is accurate and that they have the necessary rights or permission to provide such information.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure. However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

KUSHINARA CONNECT is intended for general audiences and is not specifically directed toward children under the applicable age of digital consent in their jurisdiction.

11. External Links

Our website may provide links to external websites for convenience, such as local businesses, travel resources or third-party services.

Once you leave KUSHINARA CONNECT, the external website's own policies apply.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, legal requirements or advertising practices.
