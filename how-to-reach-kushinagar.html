How to Reach Kushinagar

⬅ Back to Blog

Kushinagar is an important Buddhist pilgrimage destination in Uttar Pradesh and attracts visitors from India and other countries. You can plan your journey by air, train or road, depending on your starting city, budget and preferred travel time.

At a Glance

By Air: Kushinagar has its own international airport, while Gorakhpur is another useful airport option for travellers who continue by road.

By Rail: Gorakhpur is a major railhead commonly used for reaching Kushinagar. Padrauna also has a railway station with limited train services.

By Road: Kushinagar is connected by road with Gorakhpur and other cities in Uttar Pradesh and neighbouring states.

Before You Travel

Check your train or flight schedule before leaving, especially if your itinerary depends on a same-day connection.

For the final local journey, keep some time for road travel from the airport or railway station to your hotel and sightseeing areas.

01 • By Air

Reach Kushinagar by Flight

Kushinagar International Airport is located in Kushinagar and is the most direct airport option when a suitable flight is available for your route. The Airports Authority of India lists Kushinagar as an international Buddhist pilgrimage centre and provides airport information for travellers.

After arriving, you can continue to your hotel or sightseeing locations by local taxi or other available road transport. It is a good idea to confirm your onward transport in advance if you arrive late or have a tight itinerary.

02 • Alternative Airport

Via Gorakhpur Airport

If a suitable flight to Kushinagar is not available from your departure city, Gorakhpur Airport can be considered as an alternative. The Airports Authority of India states that Gorakhpur Airport is on the Gorakhpur–Kushinagar Highway (NH-28), and taxi and bus services are available outside the airport's main gate.

From Gorakhpur, the remaining journey to Kushinagar is by road. This option can be useful for travellers who find better flight or connection choices through Gorakhpur.

03 • By Rail

Reach Kushinagar by Train

The District Kushinagar administration identifies Gorakhpur Railway Station as a major railhead for reaching Kushinagar. Its official travel guide states that Gorakhpur railway station is about 51 km from Kushinagar and is connected with several major cities.

For some travellers, Padrauna railway station may also be useful because some trains stop there. Train availability and stopping patterns can change, so check the current railway schedule before booking.

04 • By Road

Reach Kushinagar by Bus, Taxi or Car

Road travel is a practical option for visitors coming from nearby cities. The District Kushinagar administration says the area is connected by road with places including Gorakhpur, Varanasi, Lucknow, Kanpur, Patna and Jhansi, with bus services including Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation services.

You can also travel by private car, taxi or other local road transport. If you are travelling with family or carrying luggage, a private vehicle or pre-arranged taxi can make the final part of the journey more convenient.

05 • From Gorakhpur

Gorakhpur to Kushinagar

Gorakhpur is one of the main transport gateways for visitors travelling to Kushinagar. You can arrive in Gorakhpur by train or flight and then continue by road.

From Gorakhpur Railway Station

After reaching Gorakhpur railway station, arrange a taxi, bus or other suitable road transport for the onward journey to Kushinagar.

From Gorakhpur Airport

AAI states that taxi and bus services are available outside the airport's main gate. You can continue toward Kushinagar by road.

06 • Local Travel

Getting Around Kushinagar

Once you arrive in Kushinagar, many important Buddhist sites are located within the same general tourism area. Local taxis, auto-rickshaws and other road transport can be used depending on availability and your itinerary.

For a comfortable sightseeing day, plan your route in advance and group nearby attractions together. Keep additional time for prayer, meditation, photography and visits to monasteries.

07 • Simple Travel Plans

Which Route Should You Choose?

By Flight

Consider Kushinagar International Airport when a suitable flight is available for your starting city.

By Train

Use Gorakhpur as a major rail gateway, then continue to Kushinagar by road.

By Road

Choose bus, taxi or private car when travelling from nearby cities or when you prefer a direct road journey.

Travel Tips

Things to Check Before Leaving

• Check the latest flight and train timings before booking.

• Keep your hotel address and contact number saved offline.

• If you arrive at night, confirm your onward taxi or accommodation check-in in advance.

• Carry comfortable footwear because Buddhist sites and heritage areas may involve walking.

• Keep local transport time in your itinerary instead of planning sightseeing too tightly.

• Respect temple rules, photography restrictions and quiet areas at religious sites.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Kushinagar have an airport?

Yes. Kushinagar has Kushinagar International Airport. Flight schedules should be checked for your travel date before planning the complete journey.

Which railway station is commonly used?

Gorakhpur Railway Station is a major railhead used to reach Kushinagar. Padrauna is another station that may be useful for some routes.

Can I reach Kushinagar by bus?

Yes. The District Kushinagar travel information notes road connectivity and bus services, including Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation services.

Is Gorakhpur useful for reaching Kushinagar?

Yes. Gorakhpur is an important transport gateway because it has both a major railway station and an airport, followed by a road journey to Kushinagar.
